How the fuzzy engine decides

Soybean symptoms overlap and vary in strength, so SoyDoc uses a Mamdani fuzzy inference system rather than yes or no rules.

1. Fuzzification

Each severity from 0 to 1 gets a degree of Low, Moderate and High using triangular sets that overlap.

2. Rule evaluation

IF-THEN rules per disease combine key symptoms with AND (minimum). Example: IF leaf spots are High AND yellowing is High AND pustules are High THEN soybean rust is High.

3. Aggregation

Each disease's fired rules are merged into one fuzzy output shape.

4. Defuzzification

The centroid gives a 0 to 100 score. The highest score is the likely diagnosis, linked to treatment in the database.

Input membership functions

Low 0 to 0.4, Moderate 0.3 to 0.7, High 0.6 to 1.0, with overlap so a symptom can belong partly to two sets.

Treatment advice in this prototype is general guidance compiled from soybean pathology literature. Have a plant pathologist review rules and treatments before field use.
